# How to Prepare Your Skin for Summer: 4 Essential Prep Steps

Looking for effective ways on **how to prepare your skin for summer**? Summertime is the ultimate season to get outside and bask in the glory of the warming sunshine. However, because of increased sun exposure, it’s fair to say that you need to pay a little more attention to your skincare regime compared to spring or autumn. You don’t need anyone to tell you just how damaging prolonged exposure to the sun can be on your face and body, as noted by experts at the [Skin Cancer Foundation](https://www.skincancer.org). If you want to enjoy summer to the max with the least amount of skin damage possible, ensuring your skin is properly prepped is essential. Here are four essential **ways to prep your skin for summer** so you stay radiant and protected all season long.

## 1. Even out Your Skin Tone

This is one of the best **ways to prep your skin for summer**. When the sun is out, you are much more likely to tan, and when you tan, the pigmentation differences in your skin become much more accentuated. In order to counterbalance this, try your best to clear up any lingering acne marks and hyperpigmentation on your face before spending extended hours outdoors. That way, when your skin catches the sun, those areas will not look noticeably darker than the rest of your face and body. This step works best when combined with efforts to [heal dry skin from winter](#3) to ensure a smooth, balanced canvas.

To help balance your complexion effectively, incorporate these key daily ingredients:

- **Vitamin C:** Fades dark spots and boosts radiance.
- **Niacinamide:** Calms inflammation and reduces redness.
- **Gentle Exfoliants:** Removes dead skin cells to reveal fresh skin.

## 2. Smooth Roughness

The sun can be tough on your skin through prolonged exposure, so you should work to eliminate and smooth any roughness that you have before the full effect of a summer heatwave strikes. If you have a few extra dollars to spare, one way to achieve an amazing smoothness on your face is by splashing out for professional laser treatment procedures or chemical peels. According to dermatological insights from the [American Academy of Dermatology](https://www.aad.org), these treatments trigger collagen production in your skin, which is the key to maintaining a smooth, plump, and youthful appearance. Taking these steps alongside your routine to [even out your skin tone](#1) will give you the healthiest glow possible.

## 3. Heal Dry Skin from Winter

The main problem with skin coming out of winter is that it can get severely dry and damaged from harsh environmental factors like strong winds and dry, cold air. As the summer sun comes out, any skin that remains dry will look flaky and take on an almost grey, dull tone. Make sure that you moisturise thoroughly in preparation for the hotter weather coming back to town, prioritizing products enriched with hyaluronic acid and ceramides as recommended by [Healthline Skincare](https://www.healthline.com). Hydrating your barrier now will also prepare your body for upcoming [hair removal](#4) sessions without irritation.

Here is a quick breakdown of seasonal skin hydration needs:

| Focus Area | Winter Impact | Summer Preparation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skin Barrier | Dehydrated & Flaky | Deep Moisture & Hyaluronic Acid |
| Texture | Rough & Dull | Gentle Exfoliation & Hydration |
| Protection | Windburn Defense | SPF & Barrier Support |

## 4. Hair Removal

It’s not just the skin on your face that you should be thinking about in this scenario! When bikini season comes around, it’s time to start caring for your body and downstairs area again with a proper hair removal and maintenance routine. Don’t leave this until the last minute, because issues like a wax rash, razor burn, or ingrown hairs aren’t the best look for the beach! Allow a day or two between shaving or waxing and heading into the sun so your skin has adequate time to calm down and heal.
