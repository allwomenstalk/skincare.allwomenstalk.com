# A Beginner Friendly Way to Start Using Oil Blotting Sheets

If you have oily skin, high-quality [managing oily skin](https://skincare.allwomenstalk.com/easy-ways-to-get-rid-of-oily-skin/) routine is incomplete without the right tools. You can tote these handy papers anywhere to remove excess sebum and keep your skin matte and lovely for hours. If you’re new to using **blotting paper for oily skin** or are looking to switch brands, check out my curated list of the **best blotting sheets** to combat a shiny complexion and welcome a fresh, matte finish. Finding the **best blotting paper** can truly transform your daily skincare routine, making it easy to touch up on the go. Integrating these into your routine can make oil control much easier, especially when you're looking for [luxurious abaca leaf papers](#7) or [reliable drugstore sheets](#4).

## 1. Boscia Fashionably Matte

![Boscia Fashionably Matte](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/01/1_boscia-fashionably-matte_250x250.jpg)

While Boscia's beloved green tea blotting linens are no longer available, you can easily replace them with a top-rated modern alternative like the [Fenty Beauty Invisimatte Blotting Paper](https://fentybeauty.com). This refillable blotting paper comes in a chic, lipstick-style roll that fits perfectly in any small purse or makeup bag. The continuous roll allows you to pull and tear the exact amount of paper you need, absorbing oil without disturbing your makeup or leaving a powdery residue. It’s an essential tool for keeping your complexion matte and shine-free throughout the day.

## 2. E.l.f. Shine Eraser Oil Blotting Sheets

![E.l.f. Shine Eraser Oil Blotting Sheets](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/01/2_e-l-f-shine-eraser-oil-blotting-sheets_410x410.jpg)

For an incredibly budget-friendly option, [e.l.f. Cosmetics](https://www.elfcosmetics.com) offers fantastic, inexpensive beauty products that get the job done. Their **blotting sheets** are a staple for anyone looking to control shine on a budget, which is a key step in a complete [skincare routine for oily skin](https://skincare.allwomenstalk.com/skincare-routine-for-oily-skin/). Infused with green tea extract, these sheets successfully absorb excess sebum and oil without over-drying or stripping the skin. While these paper-thin sheets can occasionally rip if you're too rough, they are lightweight, travel-friendly, and you honestly can't beat the price for such effective oil control.

## 3. Shiseido Pureness Oil-Control Blotting Paper

![Shiseido Pureness Oil-Control Blotting Paper](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/01/3_shiseido-pureness-oil-control-blotting-paper_248x168.jpg)

While Shiseido's legacy 'Pureness' line has been updated, their classic [Shiseido Oil-Control Blotting Paper](https://www.shiseido.com) remains the gold standard for heavy-duty oil absorption. These soft, gentle sheets are coated with a light, light-diffusing powder (using their signature oil-absorbing technology) that instantly neutralizes shine and leaves a fresh, matte finish. It is one of the best oil-absorbing products on the market, helping your makeup stay flawless and fresh for hours. If you struggle with a very shiny T-zone, this is well worth the investment. If you prefer a budget-friendly option to these premium sheets, you can check out the [affordable green tea sheets](#2).

## 4. Clean & Clear Instant Oil-Absorbing Sheets

![Clean & Clear Instant Oil-Absorbing Sheets](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/01/4_clean-clear-instant-oil-absorbing-sheets_410x410.jpg)

[Clean & Clear Instant Oil-Absorbing Sheets](https://www.cleanandclear.com/oil-absorbing-sheets) are a classic drugstore favorite for a reason. Unlike traditional paper-based options, these unique blue sheets have a soft, rubbery, and silky film texture that specifically targets sebum without wiping away your makeup. As you press the sheet to your face, the paper turns translucent, showing you exactly how much oil it has removed. They leave your skin feeling incredibly clean and dry, making them some of the most reliable **clean and clear blotting papers** you can find. If you are wondering whether using **oil blotting sheets is good or bad** for your skin, the answer is simple: when used correctly by gently patting the skin rather than rubbing, they are an excellent, non-comedogenic way to keep your complexion clear.

## 5. Sephora Collection Blotting Papers

![Sephora Collection Blotting Papers](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/01/5_sephora-collection-blotting-papers_240x192.jpg)

The [Sephora Collection Blotting Papers](https://www.sephora.com) are perfect for those who want specialized skincare benefits while controlling shine. Sephora offers various types of sheets infused with active ingredients to target specific skin concerns, such as clarifying tea tree oil, soothing lavender, or mattifying charcoal. These high-performing sheets do an amazing job of absorbing oil from your pores while delivering nourishing extras. They are a must-have addition to any oily skin makeup routine.

When choosing the right sheets, consider these factors:

- **Infused ingredients**: Tea tree helps clarify, while lavender soothes sensitive skin.
- **Powder coating**: Ideal for a velvet-matte finish, though plain sheets are better for pure oil absorption.
- **Portability**: Look for compact packaging that easily fits into your daily handbag.

## 6. NYX Cosmetics Blemish Control Blotting Paper

![NYX Cosmetics Blemish Control Blotting Paper](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/01/6_nyx-cosmetics-blemish-control-blotting-paper_200x136.jpg)

[NYX Professional Makeup](https://www.nyxcosmetics.com) is another beloved brand that delivers high-quality beauty products at pocket-friendly prices. Their blotting paper collection includes several targeted options, including green tea, tea tree, and blemish-controlling papers. These sheets help absorb excess sebum, reduce the appearance of enlarged pores, and keep your skin clean, clear, and healthy. They are perfect for on-the-go touch-ups when your skin starts to look a bit too shiny, especially if you want to maintain a [flawless matte makeup look](https://skincare.allwomenstalk.com/flawless-makeup-tips-for-oily-skin/).

## 7. Tatcha Aburatorigami Japanese Beauty Papers Duo

![Tatcha Aburatorigami Japanese Beauty Papers Duo](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/01/7_tatcha-aburatorigami-japanese-beauty-papers-duo_250x250.jpg)

Widely considered the ultimate luxury option, the [Tatcha Aburatorigami Japanese Blotting Papers](https://www.tatcha.com) are inspired by traditional Japanese beauty rituals. Made from 100% natural abaca leaf, these artisanal sheets are soft, biodegradable, and completely powder-free. They are famous for containing real gold flakes, which have been used for centuries to promote a radiant, exquisite complexion. The sheets gently lift away excess oil and sebum without stripping the skin's essential moisture or disturbing your foundation. For a more budget-conscious alternative that still delivers incredible results, you might also like the [classic blue sheets](#4).

For anyone dealing with excess shine, **oil blotting sheets** are an absolute lifesaver. With so many different types available, there is an option to suit every skin type and preference — from budget drugstore classics to luxurious abaca leaf sheets. Keeping a pack in your bag is the easiest way to maintain a fresh, matte complexion throughout the day without having to cake on extra face powder.

When using blotting sheets, follow these tips to get the best results:

- **Pat, don't rub**: Gently press the paper onto shiny areas to avoid wiping away your makeup.
- **Focus on the T-zone**: Target your forehead, nose, and chin where oil builds up the most.
- **Don't over-blot**: Blotting a few times a day is plenty to keep shine at bay without drying out your skin.
