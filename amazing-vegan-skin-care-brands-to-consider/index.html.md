# 8 Best Vegan Skincare Brands for Sensitive and Natural Skin

Have you ever thought about making the switch to **vegan skincare brands**? If so, you are in luck! We have curated eight amazing plant-based beauty lines that are absolutely worth trying, whether you follow a strict vegan lifestyle or simply want cleaner, toxin-free products. It is a very common misconception that vegan skincare products are not as effective as mainstream commercial alternatives, but that could not be further from the truth. In reality, natural vegan skincare formulas are exceptionally gentle for sensitive skin, free from harsh synthetic chemicals, and packed with bio-active botanicals that deeply nourish your complexion.

When upgrading your routine with clean beauty, keep these key benefits in mind:

- **Clean & Non-Toxic:** Formulated without parabens, sulfates, synthetic dyes, or harsh artificial preservatives.

- **Safe for Sensitive Skin:** Rich in soothing botanical extracts that calm inflammation and support your natural skin barrier (similar to [specialized barrier-repair options](#5)).

- **100% Cruelty-Free:** Formulated without animal testing or animal-derived ingredients.

Here is a quick overview of top-performing vegan skincare brands and what makes them stand out:

Read on to discover 8 of the greatest vegan skincare brands around to elevate your daily glow!

## 1 Ecco Bella

Vegan skin care brand [Ecco Bella](https://www.eccobella.com/) has a large collection of organic and vegan makeup, skin care and hair care. The president of Ecco Bella states that the brand is not only fab for you and the environment but it can also help you save money since many of the products multi-task! Their website is literally a one-stop shop for all thing vegan! If you are looking to build a minimalist regimen, their multi-use creams and treatments pair wonderfully with [affordable family-friendly essentials](#8) for complete head-to-toe care.

## 2 DeVita

DeVita is one of those skin care brands that takes veganism very seriously! Their site even has a list of common animal-derived ingredients and their alternatives so that you can make the choice to avoid products with any type of animal ingredients. [DeVita Natural Skin Care](https://devitaskincare.com/) offers a full range of skin care and makeup and even their boxes and inserts are 100% recycled! This high standard of ingredient transparency makes them a fantastic partner alongside [sensitive skin formulations](#5) when customizing your daily routine.

## 3 Tao

Vegan skin care that’s affordable is hard to find and while Tao skin care don’t offer drugstore prices, you can still find lots of products under ! Choose from nourishing serums, masks and even massage butters. Tao’s labs are even a green energy-neutral facility that uses renewable energy like solar and wind power to create their line of vegan and non-toxic skin care! Their commitment to clean energy perfectly complements [multi-tasking clean products](#1) for an eco-friendly regimen.

## 4 SKIN All Natural

Top vegan skin care brands also include SKIN all natural. This vegan and 100% natural company uses full-spectrum bio-active botanical extracts and minerals in all of their products. SKIN all natural says that each and every single one of their ingredients benefits your skin so you’re not paying for any fillers when you buy their products! For anyone seeking potent botanical skincare, combining these rich extracts with [clean allergen-free formulas](#7) ensures maximum skin health without irritation.

## 5 Hylunia

This next vegan skin care brand offers some fantastic options for ladies with special skin care needs. [Hylunia Skincare](https://www.hylunia.com/) says their skin care products are safe for every skin type so if you have sun damage, combo skin, skin damage from chemotherapy and more, you can use their products with confidence! You can even take a peek at all their ingredients on their website so you know what you’re buying! Their focus on ultimate gentle care works seamlessly with [transparent ingredients list standards](#2) to give you complete peace of mind.

## 6 Aubrey Organics

Aubrey Organics is one of the most recognized vegan skin care brands out there. Though the brand isn’t 100% vegan, they do have some hair care products with animal products in them, they still carry tons of fab vegan skin care products! This brand was the first to list all of their ingredients and was the first to be certified as an organic processor! Their pioneering organic formulas pair excellently with [botanical extract blends](#4) for holistic skin nutrition.

## 7 Ceela Naturals

Ceela Naturals offers vegan skin care that’s free of soy, nut, dairy and gluten! The company says that their products are so natural, they would eat the natural ingredients in their skin care creams, how awesome is that?! I’m not recommending that anyone takes a taste, but their super natural skin care products are definitely worth a try!

## 8 Jason

Vegan skin care brand [JĀSÖN Personal Care](https://www.jason-personalcare.com/) has been blazing the natural skin care trail since 1959. They offer everything from toothpaste, skin care, deodorant and even some cool stuff just for kids! Jason is very affordable so you can get something for everyone in your family and feel like a boss for saving money and saving your skin!

Vegan skin care brands are becoming more common nowadays, which is great for our animal friends and our skin! Try out some of these awesome brands and see the difference in your skin from using non-toxic and plant-based products! Have you ever tried vegan skin care products before?
