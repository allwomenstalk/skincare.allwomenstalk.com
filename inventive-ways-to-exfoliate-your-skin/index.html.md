# I Tested Every Method for Skin Exfoliation: Here’s What Worked

There are lots of different ways to exfoliate your skin, whether you're wondering what to use for exfoliation on your face, your back, arms, or legs. From natural exfoliants and gentle brushes to specialized masks and kitchen staples, discovering what to use to exfoliate skin comes down to finding what works best for your unique skin type. Whether your skin is dry, oily, sensitive, or a combination, finding the best ways to exfoliate skin will help you achieve a smooth, healthy glow. With winter weather often causing dry skin everywhere, check out these inventive skin exfoliation techniques to refresh your skincare routine from head to toe!

- **Physical Exfoliants:** Oatmeal, loofah sponges, dry brushes, pumice stones, and sugar scrubs.
- **Chemical & Natural Acid Exfoliants:** Lactic acid milk baths, salicylic acid, and alpha hydroxy acid masks.

## 1. Oatmeal Exfoliants

There are lots of great ways to exfoliate your skin using oatmeal. You can make a soothing oatmeal scrub, a hydrating facial mask, or a creamy wash. You can even take a warm oatmeal bath to soothe itchy, dry skin all over your body. Oats are naturally coarse without being uncomfortably rough, making them one of the gentlest natural things to exfoliate with. They scrub away dead skin cells and debris while releasing soothing compounds that calm inflammation. You can use them anywhere on your body to give your skin a fresh, healthy glow, similar to how [gritty washes](#6) brighten your complexion.

## 2. Milk Baths

Milk is a surprisingly inventive way to exfoliate your skin, working especially well for dry skin that needs new life. Given harsh winter weather, almost anyone can benefit from a milk soak. Whole milk is ideal because it contains lactic acid, an alpha hydroxy acid that gently dissolves dead skin cell bonds. While preparing a full milk bath might feel impractical unless you are indulging in an at-home spa day, a targeted partial bath or compress works wonderfully. Simply saturate a soft cloth or towel in whole milk, place it over problem areas for about five minutes, and gently wipe down your skin. For further guidance on safe exfoliation, consult the [American Academy of Dermatology](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-secrets/routine/safely-exfoliate-at-home) guidelines.

## 3. Loofahs

Do loofahs exfoliate skin effectively? Absolutely! Loofah sponges really exfoliate your skin and are especially beneficial for rougher patches like your knees, heels, and elbows, as well as hard-to-reach spots on your back. Because loofahs are completely natural plant fibers, they are perfect if you want to avoid synthetic chemicals or heavy scrubs. When learning how to exfoliate legs with a loofah or how to exfoliate with a loofah in the shower, simply combine it with warm water and your favorite gentle body wash. As long as you scrub gently in circular motions, it will sweep away old, dull skin cells effortlessly. If you want to compare wet scrubbing tools, consider trying [dry brushes](#4) as another great option for your arms and legs.

## 4. Dry Brushes

Dry brushes work incredibly well too, making this one of the few skin exfoliation techniques you can easily do right before your morning shower when you're in a hurry. When learning how to exfoliate arms and legs, dry brushing with a natural-bristle brush works wonders. Beyond removing dead surface skin, many people use dry brushing to boost lymphatic drainage and try to eliminate or reduce the appearance of cellulite. While results on cellulite vary, it definitely helps clear visible dry skin on arms and legs quickly, leaving your skin soft and energized.

| Exfoliation Method | Best For | Key Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oatmeal Scrubs | Sensitive & Dry Skin | Gentle, anti-inflammatory smoothing |
| Loofah Sponges | Knees, Elbows & Back | Deep physical exfoliation |
| Dry Brushing | Arms & Legs | Quick dry exfoliation & circulation boost |

## 5. Facial Masks

One of the best ways to exfoliate your skin on your face involves using a targeted facial mask. Professional over-the-counter exfoliants containing salicylic acid (BHA) or glycolic acid (AHA) are easily accessible and penetrate deep into pores to dissolve oil and dead skin cells. According to [Healthline's guide on skin exfoliation](https://www.healthline.com/health/how-to-exfoliate), chemical exfoliants are often gentler on facial skin than harsh physical scrubs. If you prefer natural DIY methods, there is a bevy of homemade masks using ingredients like honey, yogurt, or finely ground oats, similar to the [oatmeal exfoliants](#1) mentioned earlier.

## 6. Gritty Washes

There are lots of everyday items right in your kitchen that you can use as an exfoliator to refresh your skin. Fine sea salt and brown sugar both make excellent body washes that leave your skin brighter, smoother, and more vibrant at practically no cost. Sand scrubs and mineral rubs are likewise beneficial for tough areas, bringing a spa-like feel right into your home bathroom. Just remember to use fine grains and gentle pressure so you don't irritate your skin.

## 7. Pumice Stone

Finally, a pumice stone is one of the best tools to use when you need to exfoliate rough, calloused skin on your feet. Using a pumice stone regularly helps keep callouses at bay, especially if you love walking barefoot during warmer months. Keeping your feet smooth isn't just about aesthetics—it also protects your skin health when cold weather hits. Hands and feet can develop deep, painful cracks in freezing weather, so smooth away rough callouses with a damp pumice stone after a bath, and immediately lock in moisture with a rich foot cream or body butter.

Whether you prefer DIY kitchen techniques or specialized skincare tools, there are plenty of different ways to exfoliate your skin. If you're looking for what to use to exfoliate your body effectively, try experimenting with these methods; your skin will let you know what it loves best. You'll be able to tell the winner by your soft, radiant, and smooth skin! What are some of your favorite skin exfoliation techniques?
