# The Correct Order to Apply Skincare Products: 8 Essential Steps for Maximum Results

Have you ever wondered about the correct order to apply skincare products to get the best possible results from your daily routine? Most of us can probably go through our skincare steps with our eyes closed. However, applying your face products in the proper order to apply skincare makes a massive difference in how deeply active ingredients penetrate and how well your skin barrier stays protected. If you have ever asked yourself what order do you put serum and moisturizer on, or how to get the most out of your investment, this guide breaks down the correct way to apply skincare step by step. Take a look at these 8 tips on layering skincare products so you can maximize your glow every morning and night!

## 1. Cleanse

The very first step in how to apply skin care products starts with a fresh, clean canvas. Make sure you use a gentle cleanser to wash your face thoroughly, removing all traces of dirt, makeup, sunscreen, and excess oil without stripping your natural skin barrier. Keep in mind that you want to be gentle when you wash your face—avoid pulling or abrasively scrubbing your skin with harsh washcloths.

When choosing a gentle cleanser for step one, look for formulas that feature:

- Hydrating ingredients like glycerin or hyaluronic acid that preserve moisture
- Sulfate-free cleansing agents that clean effectively without causing irritation
- Balanced pH levels suited for daily morning and nighttime use

After you finish cleansing, follow up with a soothing, alcohol-free toner if you use one in your routine to balance your skin tone and pH before moving to active treatments.

## 2. Medicate

Next up on this list of skin care steps is to apply any targeted acne treatments, spot treatments, or prescription medicated creams. Board-certified dermatologist [Dr. Susan C. Taylor](https://www.aad.org/) recommends applying medicated treatments directly to clean skin so active ingredients penetrate deeply without having to fight through heavy creams or oils. Often, people think a topical treatment isn’t working, but the issue is simply that it was applied in the wrong order over a thick moisturizer! Give your spot treatment a minute to absorb before moving on to lightweight hydration like [serum application](#3).

## 3. Serum

When layering skin care products, make sure you apply your serum before your moisturizer. Skincare experts from brands like [Physicians Formula](https://www.physiciansformula.com/) note that serums contain highly concentrated active ingredients—such as vitamin C, niacinamide, or peptide complexes—designed to sink deeply into the epidermis. Because serums have smaller molecule sizes, they must be applied directly after cleansing and medicating. Additionally, since certain potent serums can feel lightweight or slightly drying on their own, you will want to follow up your [serum](#3) step with a rich, soothing cream.

## 4. Moisturize

The next step in how to apply skin care products is to apply moisturizer. Dermatologist [Dr. Amy Wechsler](https://dramywechsler.com/) highlights that while moisturizer cannot permanently change your genetics, it plays a vital role in locking in hydration and strengthening your skin's natural barrier function. Using moisturizer regularly prevents trans-epidermal water loss and helps maintain a radiant, youthful glow. After letting your serum absorb, smooth moisturizer evenly across your face and neck. For optimal protection during the day, make sure your moisture layer sets before applying your [sunscreen shield](#5).

## 5. Protect

This next tip in skin care steps has been a popular topic of debate among beauty enthusiasts. Some skin experts suggest applying sunscreen prior to moisturizer, while others recommend layering it as the final step in your daytime routine. The distinction comes down to the formula: if you use a chemical sunscreen, it needs to absorb into clean skin, whereas a physical (mineral) sunscreen acts as a reflective shield on top of your skincare.

Here are quick rules for choosing between sunscreen types:

- **Chemical Sunscreens:** Formulated with organic compounds like avobenzone; best applied before heavy creams so active ingredients absorb.
- **Physical Sunscreens:** Formulated with zinc oxide or titanium dioxide; applied as the final daytime step to reflect UV rays.

To avoid confusion entirely, consider using a dual-action moisturizing SPF that hydrates while providing broad-spectrum protection.

## 6. Nourish

If you are following these skin care steps in the evening, your nighttime routine skips daytime SPF in favor of targeted eye creams and restorative night oils. You have likely heard it many times, but it is best to pat eye creams gently using your ring finger—the weakest finger—since the skin around your eyes is exceptionally thin and delicate. Avoid pulling, dragging, or stretching the skin in this area to prevent premature sagging, and apply your eye product along the orbital bone. For evening routines, ensure your eye cream is applied before heavier night creams so it absorbs fully into delicate tissues, keeping your skin barrier healthy just like your [moisturizer layer](#4).

## 7. Light to Heavy

One of the rules to remember in how to apply skin care products is to always apply the lightest, thinnest water-based products first, gradually building up to heavier, oil-based creams and ointments last. If you reverse the process and put dense creams on first, lighter serums and treatments cannot penetrate the lipid barrier to do their job. Heavier formulations can easily pass through lightweight layers, so starting thinnest to thickest guarantees every product performs at its best, starting right from your initial [cleansing step](#1).

## 8. Give It Time

When you’re layering skin care products, make sure you give each layer enough time to absorb into your skin before moving to the next. In busy mornings or late evenings, it is tempting to rush through your routine or slap multiple face products on all at once. However, rushing causes pilling and reduces product potency. Allowing 30 to 60 seconds between steps—especially after applying active serums or waiting for your [moisturizer application](#4)—gives your skin time to drink in each formula for maximum skincare benefits.

I hope these tips on how to apply skin care products were helpful for you! While dermatologists offer clear principles on product order, skincare is ultimately personal. Try these 8 steps out and see what combination delivers the best results for your unique skin type. How do you like to layer your skin care?
