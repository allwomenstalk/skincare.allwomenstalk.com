# I Tested 8 Bikini Season Skincare Products So You Don’t Have To

Is your bathroom stocked with essential [skincare products](https://skincare.allwomenstalk.com/) for swimsuit weather? While it might feel early to start thinking about beach days, preparing your skin ahead of time makes all the difference. Getting smooth, glowing skin for bikini season doesn’t have to feel like a daunting chore. With the right routine, you can easily achieve a sun-kissed, hydrated look that lasts all summer long.

| Product Category | Key Benefit | Ideal Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bronzers & Tanners | Natural streak-free glow | Full Body |
| Sun Protection | Water-resistant UV shield | Face & Body |
| Exfoliating Scrubs | Smooths rough, dry patches | Legs & Feet |

## 1. St. Tropez Self Tan Bronzing Lotion

![St. Tropez Self Tan Bronzing Lotion](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/03/1_st-tropez-self-tan-bronzing-lotion_250x250.jpg)

Self-tanners top the list of essentials for swimsuit season. A quality bronzer gives skin an instant radiant glow while helping even out skin tone. [St. Tropez](https://www.sttropeztan.com) produces a top-rated line of self-tanning lotions known for delivering natural-looking results without streaks. If you prefer lightweight application, they also offer easy-to-use bronzing mousses and sprays.

## 2. Supergoop! Everyday Face & Body Lotion with Parsol 1789 plus Broad Spectrum SPF 30 (Endless Summer Pump)

![Supergoop! Everyday Face & Body Lotion with Parsol 1789 plus Broad Spectrum SPF 30 (Endless Summer Pump)](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/03/2_supergoop-everyday-face-body-lotion-with-parsol-1789-plus-broad-spectrum-spf-30-endless-summer-pump_250x250.jpg)

Daily sun protection is non-negotiable for healthy skin year-round. [Supergoop!](https://supergoop.com) provides broad-spectrum SPF 30 protection that hydrates while defending against UV damage. The formula is lightweight, water-resistant for up to 80 minutes, and paraben-free, making it far superior to heavy, traditional sunscreens.

- Delivers broad-spectrum SPF 30 UV defense for 80 minutes
- Hydrates deeply without feeling pasty or greasy
- Clean, paraben-free formula suitable for face and body

## 3. Sally Hansen Simple Smooth Hair Remover Creme for All Skin Types

![Sally Hansen Simple Smooth Hair Remover Creme for All Skin Types](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/03/3_sally-hansen-simple-smooth-hair-remover-creme-for-all-skin-types_105x224.jpg)

If shaving leads to irritation, depilatory creams offer a smooth alternative for hair removal. [Sally Hansen](https://www.sallyhansen.com) offers an in-shower formula that works in just three minutes. For those sensitive to strong chemical scents, looking for odorless depilatory cream options like an odorless Nair or Veet formula ensures a pleasant, irritation-free experience.

## 4. Nip + Fab Dry Leg Fix

![Nip + Fab Dry Leg Fix](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/03/4_nip-fab-dry-leg-fix_300x300.jpg)

Warmer weather means your legs take center stage, making hydration essential. Before applying [self-tanning bronzers](#1), treat parched legs with an intensive moisturizer formulated to instantly smooth rough texture. Daily application locks in moisture, leaving your skin soft and glowing all day.

## 5. Neutrogena Body Clear Body Scrub

![Neutrogena Body Clear Body Scrub](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/03/5_neutrogena-body-clear-body-scrub_410x410.jpg)

Regular exfoliation keeps your skin clear and vibrant when wearing bikinis. [Neutrogena](https://www.neutrogena.com) Body Clear Body Scrub features salicylic acid to combat body breakouts while sloughing off dead skin cells, ensuring your back and shoulders stay smooth and bump-free.

## 6. Burt's Bees Sugar Scrub in Cranberry & Pomegranate

![Burt's Bees Sugar Scrub in Cranberry & Pomegranate](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/03/6_burts-bees-sugar-scrub-in-cranberry-pomegranate_401x354.jpg)

If you don't require [medicated body scrubs](#5), a nourishing sugar scrub is a luxurious option. [Burt's Bees](https://www.burtsbees.com) blends hydrating shea butter, pomegranate oil, and natural sugar crystals to gently polish skin, revealing a bright and radiant complexion.

## 7. True Blue Spa Smoothing Foot Scrub

![True Blue Spa Smoothing Foot Scrub](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/03/7_true-blue-spa-smoothing-foot-scrub_280x360.jpg)

Sandal season requires giving extra attention to neglected feet. A targeted foot scrub breaks down tough calluses and thick skin, leaving heels soft and smooth. Pair it with a rich moisturizing cream to keep your feet sandal-ready every day.

## 8. Tend Skin

![Tend Skin](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/skincare/2013/03/8_tend-skin_250x250.jpg)

If razor bumps or ingrown hairs are a concern, Tend Skin is an indispensable solution. Designed for use after shaving or waxing, this liquid treatment reduces redness and bumps within 24 to 48 hours, keeping your bikini line smooth and clear.

Prepping your skin for swimsuit season is easy when you have the right products in your routine. Combining exfoliation, hydration, and daily sun protection ensures your skin looks healthy and radiant. What are your favorite go-to products for bikini season prep?
