# 7 Natural Blemish Remedies & Treatments to Try at Home

Check out these nifty DIY blemish remedies to fight acne the cheap and easy way, all in the comfort of your own home! One of the most frustrating skin moments is waking up to a zit that popped up overnight. Fortunately, finding a natural blemish remedy or effective blemish treatment at home doesn't require expensive spa visits. After reading this guide, you will have 7 simple, natural ways to tackle breakouts and banish acne angst for good!

Before diving into each homemade blemish treatment, keep these quick best practices in mind:

- Always perform a patch test on a small area of skin before full application.
- Opt for organic, unprocessed ingredients whenever possible.
- Follow up drying treatments with a light, non-comedogenic moisturizer.

## 1. Honey

Honey is one of the top ingredients in DIY facial masks, and it also plays a major role in natural blemish remedies. While many people view honey strictly as a moisturizer, raw honey boasts powerful antibacterial and antimicrobial properties that actively clear stubborn blemishes. Simply dab a pea-sized amount of unprocessed honey onto the affected area, leave it on for about 15 minutes, and rinse thoroughly with warm water.

## 2. Vinegar and Water

An easy at-home acne treatment that balances your skin's pH levels while reducing redness and inflammation is a simple mixture of vinegar and water. Mix equal parts purified water and raw apple cider vinegar (or distilled white vinegar), saturate a clean cotton ball, and dab gently onto the blemish. Rinse off after 10 minutes to reveal calm, refreshed skin.

## 3. Tea Tree Oil Mask

Another effective way to treat blemishes treatment at home is by crafting a soothing facial mask. Spot treating works well, but occasionally your whole face benefits from a deeply clarifying mask! Combine 1 teaspoon of [raw honey](#1) with 2 drops of pure tea tree oil—which you can easily find at local health food stores. Apply evenly across your face, let it sit for 15 to 20 minutes, and rinse with lukewarm water. According to [dermatological studies](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/), tea tree oil offers potent antimicrobial activity against acne-causing bacteria.

## 4. Garlic

Garlic is a fantastic natural blemish remedy due to its potent antiseptic and antibacterial properties. Simply crush a fresh garlic clove and mix it with a ½ cup of lukewarm water. Apply the solution to problem areas with a cotton pad and rinse off completely after 5 minutes. As [dermatology experts](https://www.healthline.com/) advise, raw garlic can cause skin irritation if left on too long or applied undiluted, so proceed with care.

## 5. Aloe

A wonderful natural blemish home remedy that decreases redness and swelling is aloe vera. You can extract fresh gel directly from an aloe plant or purchase 100% pure aloe vera gel at your local health store. Smooth the gel over your entire face as a soothing mask or use it to spot treat individual breakouts. According to [NCCIH guidelines on aloe vera](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/), topical aloe is renowned for calming skin inflammation and helping fade post-acne marks over time.

| Remedy Ingredient | Key Skin Benefit | Best Application Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raw Honey | Antibacterial & Hydrating | Overnight spot treatment |
| Tea Tree Oil | Deep Antimicrobial Cleansing | Diluted facial mask |
| Aloe Vera Gel | Calming & Scar Fading | Daily moisturizer or mask |

## 6. Sea Salt

Salt might sound like an unexpected addition to a blemish home remedy list, but sea salt yields remarkable results. Warm up one cup of distilled water, dissolve 1 tablespoon of sea salt, and soak a clean washcloth or cotton ball in the solution. Apply gently to affected areas for 5 minutes. Because salt can be drying, always follow up with a [soothing aloe vera gel](#5) or non-comedogenic moisturizer.

## 7. Honey and Cinnamon

Since [honey](#1) is exceptionally soothing for the skin, it naturally makes another appearance in our DIY lineup! Combined with cinnamon, which brings oxygen and nutrients to the skin's surface, this combination creates a potent paste. Mix 2 tablespoons of raw honey with 1 teaspoon of cinnamon powder, apply as a mask or spot treatment, and rinse after 20 minutes. Limit this mask to once a week to prevent skin sensitivity.

These DIY blemish remedies can all be made in a flash using simple household ingredients! As with any new skincare application, test each remedy on a small patch of skin first. Which of these natural blemish treatments are you excited to try first?
